Q: How long do I have to return a battery, and how must it be packed?
A: [Returns] Unused products can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Batteries must be returned in their original packaging. [Shipping] Orders over 100 euro ship free within the EU in 3 to 5 working days. Batteries ship by ground freight only. [Warranty] E-bike frames and motors carry a 5 year warranty. Batteries carry a 2 year warranty or 500 charge cycles, whichever comes first.
